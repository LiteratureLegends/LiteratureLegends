# Product

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Students and researchers collect academic papers, scan physical documents, and
organize research into personal projects. Profile is their personal workspace:
identity and account editing. This draft covers only the account Profile section;
workspace statistics and project management are outside its scope.

## Direction and sources

Follow the team's existing UI, as requested. Sources are `readme.md`,
`images/figma.png`, `images/Wireframe3.jpeg`, Jordan's PaperSearch views, and
Daniel's SwiftUI starter. Use familiar native interactions, readable research
metadata, and the calm personal-workspace direction of the mockup.

## Principles

- Make personal research easy to resume.
- Keep identity and project organization distinct.
- Match the team's native navigation and system typography.
- Keep sample data separate so backend integration can replace it.

## Anti-references

The meeting removed Community. Avoid social feeds, collaboration controls, and
chat features in this initial Profile draft. Avoid imposing a separate visual
identity on the team's established mockup.

## Accessibility

Use native controls, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, sufficient touch targets,
and semantic system colors that support dark mode. These are implementation
defaults for the draft; no additional accessibility requirements were specified.
